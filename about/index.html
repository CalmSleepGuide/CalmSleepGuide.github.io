About

A quiet field guide to sleeping better.

Updated 2026

Calm Sleep Guide is a small site about a small subject: the ordinary, unglamorous habits that help people sleep. No supplements to sell, no trackers to push, no five-step morning routines. Just clear writing about what actually moves the needle, and what doesn't.

What we write about

Wind-down routines. Light and temperature. Naps. Middle-of-the-night wakings. Travel and shift work. Sleep with kids in the house. The everyday material — written for a person, not a search engine.

How we write

Every article starts with a question we've heard from a real person and ends with something you can try tonight. We lean on peer-reviewed research where it's solid, and we say so plainly when it isn't. If a piece of advice has a caveat, the caveat is in the article — not buried in a disclaimer.

The goal isn't perfect sleep. It's a few more good nights a month than you'd otherwise have.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell products, supplements, or affiliate links.
  • We don't track you across the web. Analytics are aggregate and anonymous.
  • We don't use AI-generated cover images or filler — every article is written and edited by a person.
