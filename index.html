Simple Sleep Tips

Keep a Routine

Try going to bed and waking up at the same time every day. A steady rhythm helps your body know when it is time to rest.

Reduce Screen Time

Give yourself at least 30 minutes away from bright screens before bed. This helps your mind slow down naturally.

Create a Calm Room

A cool, dark, and quiet bedroom can make it easier to fall asleep and stay asleep through the night.
